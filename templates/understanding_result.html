Analysis Results Overview

GreenSpace AI generates comprehensive analysis results including classification maps, statistical reports, confidence layers, and accuracy metrics. This guide explains how to interpret each component and make data-driven decisions based on the results.

Classification Map

Pixel-level land cover classification showing urban areas, vegetation, water bodies, and other features with color-coded visualization.

Key Metrics:
Resolution: 1-30m Classes: 4-15 Format: GeoTIFF

Quantitative analysis including area calculations, percentage coverage, change detection statistics, and spatial pattern metrics.

Includes:
• Area by class
• Percentage distribution
• Spatial statistics
• Change metrics

Low Confidence Areas

Common causes: Mixed pixels, shadows, cloud cover, seasonal changes, or complex land cover patterns.

  • Requires manual validation
  • Consider higher resolution imagery
  • May need multi-temporal analysis

Medium Confidence Areas

Typical for: Transition zones, urban-rural interfaces, fragmented landscapes, and moderately complex areas.

  • Generally reliable for analysis
  • Spot-check recommended
  • Suitable for most applications

High Confidence Areas

Found in: Homogeneous areas like large water bodies, dense forests, urban cores, and agricultural fields.

  • Highly reliable results
  • Minimal validation needed
  • Suitable for decision-making

Urban Areas Interpretation

Characteristics: High reflectance in all bands, geometric patterns, road networks, building shadows.

Common Confusions:
• Bare soil in construction sites
• Concrete surfaces vs. bare rock
• Mixed urban-vegetation pixels

Vegetation Analysis

NDVI Range: Healthy vegetation: 0.3-0.8, Stressed vegetation: 0.1-0.3, Non-vegetation: <0.1.

Seasonal Considerations:
• Leaf-on vs leaf-off seasons
• Crop rotation patterns
• Drought stress indicators

Water Body Detection

Spectral Signature: Low reflectance in NIR, high absorption, smooth texture, distinct edges.

Potential Errors:
• Shadows misclassified as water
• Turbid vs clear water
• Shallow water detection
