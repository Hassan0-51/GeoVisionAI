Executive Summary

Faisalabad, known as the "Manchester of Pakistan," has experienced rapid industrial growth over the past decade. This study assesses the environmental impact of this development, focusing on green space loss, urban heat island intensification, and air quality degradation. The analysis provides evidence-based recommendations for sustainable industrial development.

Critical Finding

Industrial expansion has led to an 8.3% reduction in green spaces (2010-2023), contributing to a 1.8°C temperature increase in affected areas and significant air quality deterioration.
