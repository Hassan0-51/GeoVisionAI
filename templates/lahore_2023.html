Lahore Urban Green Space Assessment 2023

Comprehensive analysis of Lahore's green spaces and their cooling effects using satellite imagery and deep learning

Study Period
January - December 2023
Location
Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan
Collaborators
University of the Punjab, LDA
Data Sources
Sentinel-2, MODIS, Landsat 8

Executive Summary

This comprehensive study analyzed Lahore's urban green spaces using high-resolution satellite imagery and deep learning models. The research aimed to quantify green space distribution, assess cooling effects, and provide data-driven recommendations for urban planning and climate resilience.

Key Takeaway

Lahore has 18.7% green coverage, providing average temperature reduction of 3.2°C in vegetated areas. However, distribution is uneven, with 65% of green spaces concentrated in only 30% of the city area.

Methodology

Data Collection & Processing

The study utilized Sentinel-2 satellite imagery (10m resolution) for the entire Lahore metropolitan area. Additional thermal data from MODIS and Landsat 8 were used for temperature analysis. All images were preprocessed for cloud removal and atmospheric correction using Google Earth Engine.

Analysis Approach

1
Image Segmentation
U-Net deep learning model trained on 5,000 annotated images for accurate green space identification.
2
Temperature Mapping
Land Surface Temperature (LST) calculation using thermal bands from satellite data.
3
Spatial Analysis
GIS analysis to correlate green space density with temperature patterns across Lahore.
4
Validation
Ground truth validation through field surveys at 150 locations across the city.

Policy Recommendations

High Priority

Strategic Tree Planting

Plant 500,000 native trees in heat-vulnerable areas identified through thermal analysis.

  • Focus on Zone 4 & 5 with lowest green coverage
  • Prioritize heat-tolerant native species
  • Community-based planting initiatives
High Priority

Green Infrastructure Standards

Mandate 25% green space in all new residential and commercial developments.

  • Update building codes and zoning regulations
  • Incentivize green roofs and vertical gardens
  • Establish maintenance requirements
Medium Priority

Urban Park Network

Develop 50 new neighborhood parks in underserved areas over 5 years.

  • Target areas with >30,000 residents per park
  • Minimum 2-acre size for effective cooling
  • Include water features for enhanced cooling
Medium Priority

Heat Action Plan

Develop city-wide heat mitigation strategy based on green space analysis.

  • Identify heat refuge locations
  • Establish cooling center network
  • Public awareness campaigns
