About Our Research

This platform is developed as part of a final year research project at the University of the Punjab, Lahore, under the supervision of Dr. Syed M Ali.

Our research focuses on evaluating the cooling effects of Urban Green Spaces in Lahore using state-of-the-art remote sensing and deep learning techniques.
