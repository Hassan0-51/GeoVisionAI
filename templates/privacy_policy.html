1. Overview & Scope

GreenSpace AI provides geospatial analysis services using deep learning models to process satellite imagery and geographic data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your data when you use our platform.

Important Notice

Geospatial Data Sensitivity: Uploaded satellite imagery and GIS data may contain sensitive location information. We implement enterprise-grade security measures and process data in compliance with international regulations.

Satellite Imagery Analysis

AI-powered land cover classification and feature extraction

GIS Data Processing

Vector and raster data analysis, transformation, and export

AI Model Training

Custom deep learning model development and deployment

API Services

Programmatic access to analysis capabilities

3.2 AI Model Training & Improvement

To improve our deep learning models, we may use anonymized and aggregated analysis results. This process follows strict protocols:

  • Anonymization: All location identifiers are removed before model training
  • Aggregation: Data is combined from multiple sources to prevent re-identification
  • Opt-Out: Users can opt-out of data use for model improvement in account settings
  • Research Ethics: All data use for research follows academic ethics guidelines

4.2 Algorithmic Fairness & Bias Mitigation

We implement comprehensive bias mitigation strategies:

  • Diverse Training Data: Models trained on 500,000+ images from 25 countries across 6 continents
  • Bias Audits: Quarterly algorithmic fairness assessments using standardized metrics
  • Regional Adaptation: Model fine-tuning for different geographical and cultural contexts
  • Transparency Reports: Annual publication of model performance across different regions

4.3 Model Limitations & Accuracy

Important Limitations

Accuracy Variances: Model performance varies by region, season, and data quality. Urban areas typically achieve 94-96% accuracy, while complex landscapes may show 85-90% accuracy.

Not for Critical Decisions: Our analysis should not be used as the sole basis for critical decisions (emergency response, legal boundaries, etc.) without ground validation.
