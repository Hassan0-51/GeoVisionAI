General Questions

What is GreenSpace AI?

GreenSpace AI is a cloud-based platform that uses artificial intelligence and deep learning to analyze satellite and aerial imagery. Our platform enables users to perform automated land cover classification, change detection, object identification, and custom model training for geospatial applications.

We serve researchers, environmental organizations, urban planners, agricultural companies, and government agencies who need efficient, accurate analysis of large-scale geospatial data.

Who can use GreenSpace AI?

GreenSpace AI is designed for:

  • Researchers & Academics: Environmental science, geography, urban studies
  • Government Agencies: Environmental monitoring, urban planning, disaster response
  • Environmental Organizations: Conservation, climate change research
  • Agricultural Companies: Crop monitoring, yield prediction
  • Urban Planners: Infrastructure development, land use analysis
  • Insurance Companies: Risk assessment, damage evaluation

We offer plans suitable for individual researchers up to large enterprise organizations.

Do I need GIS expertise to use the platform?

No, you don't need to be a GIS expert to use GreenSpace AI. Our platform is designed with user-friendly interfaces that guide you through the analysis process. However, having basic knowledge of geospatial concepts will help you get the most out of our platform.

For advanced users, we provide full control over analysis parameters, custom model training, and API access for integration with existing GIS workflows.

Related: Getting Started Tutorial Documentation

What satellite imagery sources do you support?

We support analysis of imagery from multiple sources:

  • Free Sources: Sentinel-2, Landsat 8/9
  • Commercial Sources: Maxar (WorldView), PlanetScope, Airbus
  • Aerial Imagery: Drone imagery, aerial photography
  • Custom Sources: Upload your own georeferenced imagery

Our platform automatically handles different resolutions, coordinate systems, and file formats. For commercial imagery, you'll need to provide your own data access credentials.

Account & Billing

How much does GreenSpace AI cost?

We offer several pricing tiers:

  • Free Tier: 50 analysis credits/month, limited features
  • Basic: $29/month - 500 credits, standard features
  • Professional: $99/month - 2,500 credits, advanced features
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing - unlimited credits, full features

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. Annual billing offers discounts of 14-20%.

Related: View Pricing Plans Contact Sales

What are analysis credits?

Analysis credits are our unit of measurement for processing work. One credit typically represents:

  • Processing 1 square kilometer of satellite imagery at 10m resolution
  • OR analyzing 1 standard-sized image tile (512x512 pixels)
  • OR 1 minute of model training on standard hardware

Credits reset monthly and unused credits roll over up to 2x your monthly limit. You can purchase additional credits if needed.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time:

  • Upgrades: Take effect immediately with pro-rated billing
  • Downgrades: Take effect at the start of your next billing cycle
  • Annual to Monthly: Available at the end of your annual term

When downgrading, make sure your current usage fits within the limits of the new plan.

Image Analysis

How accurate are your AI models?

Our pre-trained models achieve the following accuracy levels:

  • Land Cover Classification: 92-96% overall accuracy
  • Change Detection: 89-93% accuracy for significant changes
  • Building Detection: 94-97% precision
  • Road Extraction: 91-95% accuracy

Accuracy can vary based on image quality, resolution, and geographic region. For specialized applications, we recommend training custom models using your own annotated data.

Related: Custom Model Training

How long does analysis take?

Analysis time depends on several factors:

  • Image size: 1 sq km takes ~1-2 minutes
  • Complexity: More complex analyses take longer
  • Plan tier: Pro and Enterprise get priority processing
  • Current load: Server load can affect processing time

Typical processing times:

  • Small area (10 sq km): 5-10 minutes
  • Medium area (100 sq km): 30-60 minutes
  • Large area (1,000 sq km): 3-5 hours

You'll receive email notifications when analysis is complete.

What output formats do you provide?

We support multiple output formats:

  • Geospatial Formats: GeoJSON, Shapefile, KML, GeoTIFF
  • Image Formats: PNG, JPEG (with georeferencing)
  • Data Formats: CSV, Excel, JSON
  • Reports: PDF reports with statistics and visualizations
  • Interactive: Web maps, 3D visualizations

You can choose multiple output formats for each analysis. All outputs include metadata about the analysis parameters and accuracy metrics.

Can I analyze historical satellite imagery?

Yes, you can analyze historical imagery:

  • Sentinel-2: Archive back to 2015
  • Landsat: Archive back to the 1970s
  • Commercial archives: Varies by provider (contact us for details)

For change detection over time, you can specify multiple dates and our platform will automatically align and compare the imagery. Historical analysis uses the same credits as current imagery analysis.

AI Models

What AI models do you offer?

We offer several pre-trained models:

  • Land Cover Classification: 10-class classification (urban, vegetation, water, etc.)
  • Change Detection: Identify changes between two dates
  • Building Detection: Detect and segment buildings
  • Road Extraction: Extract road networks
  • Water Body Detection: Identify lakes, rivers, ponds
  • Vegetation Health: NDVI and vegetation stress analysis
  • Crop Type Classification: Identify different crop types

All models are regularly updated and improved based on new training data.

Can I train custom models?

Yes! Our custom model training feature allows you to:

  • Upload your own annotated training data
  • Choose from multiple model architectures (U-Net++, DeepLabv3+, Vision Transformers)
  • Fine-tune hyperparameters for optimal performance
  • Monitor training progress in real-time
  • Deploy trained models for inference

Custom models require Pro or Enterprise plans. We recommend at least 500 annotated images for good results.

Related: Custom Model Training Guide

How much data do I need for custom training?

Recommended dataset sizes:

  • Minimum: 200-300 annotated images
  • Good: 500-1000 annotated images
  • Excellent: 1000+ annotated images

Additional recommendations:

  • Include diverse examples (different seasons, lighting conditions)
  • Ensure balanced class distribution
  • Use precise annotations with clear boundaries
  • Include validation and test sets (20% each)

Our platform includes data augmentation to effectively multiply your training data.

API & Integration

Do you offer an API?

Yes, we offer a comprehensive REST API that allows you to:

  • Submit analysis jobs programmatically
  • Retrieve analysis results
  • Manage projects and datasets
  • Train and deploy custom models
  • Monitor usage and billing

API access is included with Pro and Enterprise plans. Basic plans can access the API with additional purchase.

Related: API Documentation

Can I integrate with QGIS or ArcGIS?

Yes, we offer integration options for both:

  • QGIS Plugin: Free plugin available in the QGIS plugin repository
  • ArcGIS Integration: Python toolbox for ArcGIS Pro
  • Web Services: WMS/WFS services for integration with any GIS software
  • Python SDK: Full-featured Python library for custom integrations

These integrations allow you to run GreenSpace AI analyses directly from your GIS software and import results seamlessly.
