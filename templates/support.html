What deep learning models does GreenSpace AI use for satellite imagery analysis?

Our platform employs multiple state-of-the-art deep learning architectures:

  • U-Net++: For high-precision semantic segmentation of land cover types
  • DeepLabv3+: For urban feature extraction with atrous convolution
  • HRNet: For maintaining high-resolution representations throughout network
  • Vision Transformers (ViT): For classification of complex land use patterns
  • Custom CNN Ensembles: Trained on our proprietary dataset of 500,000+ annotated satellite images

What satellite imagery sources are supported?

We support all major satellite imagery sources:

Sentinel-2

10m resolution, 13 spectral bands

Landsat 8/9

30m resolution, 11 spectral bands

PlanetScope

3m resolution, daily revisit

Maxar

0.3m resolution, commercial

We also support GeoTIFF, JPEG2000, and raw image uploads with proper georeferencing.
